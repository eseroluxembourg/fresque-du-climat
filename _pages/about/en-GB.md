title: 'About ClimateFrom.Space'
---

This website was created by [ESERO Luxembourg](https://www.esero.lu) to illustrate the impact of climate change using satellite images of the [European Space Agency](https://www.esa.int/).

It is based on the [Climate Fresk](https://climatefresk.org), an awareness-raising workshop where participants co-construct a fresco summarising the mechanisms of climate change as explained in the IPCC reports.

If you have noticed a bug or have a suggestion for improvement, please contact us [via email](mailto:contact@esero.lu).
