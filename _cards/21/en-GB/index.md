title: Rising Temperatures
backDescription: >-
  The average air temperature at the surface of the Earth has increased by 1.2°C since 1900.
  Future emission scenarios predict that this increase will reach between 2 and 5°C by 2100.
  During the last ice age 20,000 years ago, the average air temperature was only 6°C lower than now.
