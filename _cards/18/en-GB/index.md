  Satellites have provided a reliable tool for continuously monitoring changes in the Arctic ice since 1993.

  This visualization shows  how climate change is affecting Arctic sea ice extent over time (on left) and highlights the evolution of sea ice volume through years (on right).
  The decrease of sea ice volume is significant in all seasons.
  In 2023, Arctic minimum sea ice coverage was at its lowest on record, extending a long-term downward trend.
